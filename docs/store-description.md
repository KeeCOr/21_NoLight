# InkWarrior — Steam Store Description

## Short Description

Strike. Dodge. Finish. InkWarrior is an ink-brush action platformer where every attack paints the battlefield — stylized combat in a dark, atmospheric world presented in portrait orientation.

---

## Full Description

**InkWarrior** is a 2D action platformer built around the expressiveness of ink. You move, strike, and combo your way through a world rendered in dark tones and brushstroke geometry, where the visual language of each attack reflects the force behind it. Slash an enemy and a brush ring erupts from the impact. Chain three hits and the screen fills with ink smear trails and a heavy flash that signals you are in control.

Combat is designed around a short, readable action set: attack, evade, guard, and finish. The satisfaction comes not from complexity, but from execution — landing a three-hit combo at the exact right moment, triggering the ink burst of a finish kill, reading when to dodge versus when to hold and counter. Every input has a clear, immediate visual and audio response that confirms your read was correct before the next frame begins.

The game is presented in a 9:16 portrait layout, reflecting the design sensibility of mobile-first action games while running natively on Windows through an Electron shell. This vertical canvas puts your character at the center of a focused, cinematic frame, cutting out peripheral clutter and keeping your attention locked onto the enemy in front of you.

The world of InkWarrior is sparse but deliberate. Dark backgrounds, high-contrast ink effects, and minimal UI create an atmospheric space that makes each encounter feel weighty. This is not a game that overwhelms you with systems. It earns tension through simplicity and release through the snap of a well-timed combo.

---

## Key Features

- **Ink-Based Combat VFX** — Every attack is expressed through brushstroke visual effects. Basic strikes produce brush slash rings; combos escalate to smears, flashes, and ink bursts. The visual intensity scales with your aggression.
- **Combo Escalation System** — 1-hit, 2-hit, and 3-hit combos each trigger distinct camera, VFX, and audio responses. Mastering the rhythm of escalation is the core skill expression of the game.
- **Finish Kill Mechanics** — Depleting an enemy triggers a finish state with a distinct ink burst VFX and camera pause, rewarding precision and punishing hesitation.
- **Portrait 9:16 Layout** — Designed for a vertically-framed screen. The narrow canvas creates a focused, cinematic frame that sharpens spatial awareness and keeps combat intimate.
- **Atmospheric Dark Art Direction** — High-contrast ink aesthetics, dark backgrounds, and minimal UI keep the tone consistent and immersive across every encounter.
